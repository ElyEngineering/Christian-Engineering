Why we gather

Engineers. Disciples. Husbands in training.

Ely Christian Engineers is a small community in northern Minnesota for young Christian men who write code, design systems, and still want their homes to look more like Christ’s love for the church than like the culture’s definition of strength.

We take Ephesians 5 seriously — not as a slogan, but as a way of life. Headship without sacrifice is not biblical. Technical excellence without character is not enough. We want both: sharp minds and soft hearts under the lordship of Jesus.

What we stand for

Three commitments

Sacrificial love

Christ “gave himself up” for the church. We pursue that same posture toward our wives, fiancées, and future marriages — costly love, not convenience.

Servant leadership

Leadership that washes feet. We refuse passivity and refuse domination. We lead by initiative, protection, provision, and humility before God.

Integrity in engineering

Honest work, clean systems, truthful communication. The same character that shepherds a home should show up in the pull request and the stand-up.

Who it’s for

Built for men like you

  • Young Christian engineers (and engineering-adjacent builders) in or near Ely, MN
  • Men who want Scripture — not slogans — to shape marriage and manhood
  • Husbands, fiancés, and single men preparing to love a wife as Christ loved the church
  • Brothers who care about craftsmanship at work and faithfulness at home

Women who share this vision for biblical marriage are welcome in prayer and encouragement; the core gathering is aimed at forming men who take Ephesians 5:25 personally.
